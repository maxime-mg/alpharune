## Scope
- Both players' main deck, trash, banishment, rune deck, runes area, base, legend zone, champion zone, score track and hand, opponent mirrored at the top.
- Both battlefields in the middle with controller, contested, combat and showdown badges and face-down cards.
- Opponent's hand and decks shown as card backs with counts.

## Done when
A full game can be watched and played with every zone visible, at desktop and laptop widths.
